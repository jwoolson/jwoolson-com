Technical Report (2009)

Web Research Report

The 2009 SUNY Fredonia Web Research Report is an in-depth study which defined the institutional goals, requirements and architecture pathway for modernization of the university’s web content management environment. The study integrates governance, accessibility and UX, distributed authoring and workflow, structured/shared content, infrastructure reliability, archival preservation, and potentials for future WCM/ECM integrated web ecosystem. It provided the research foundation for development of a sustainable, university-wide Campus Web Plan.
